Sending Options

Workers multiply throughput for bulk providers. Keep it low for Gmail/Outlook to avoid rate limits. Retries only apply to transient (connection / 4xx) failures.

Deliverability

Inbox placement is decided by the receiving provider and your domain's reputation. To land in the inbox your sending domain must publish SPF, DKIM and DMARC DNS records that align with the Sender Email you enter here. Transmit already adds Date, Message-ID, Reply-To, a List-Unsubscribe header and a plain-text alternative, and it sends the envelope sender as your From address. Use a modest worker count, add a delay to respect provider limits, keep bounces low and never send to purchased lists.
